# Code of conduct

OMS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted anywhere in the project's spaces.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's GitHub profile or the contact listed on [barestate.xyz](https://barestate.xyz). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers may remove content or ban accounts that break this code.
